## 核心记忆线

1. We moved through four spaces.
2. Each space staged a different Macau.
3. Intercultural communication happened through signs, bodies, crowds, commodities, and interaction.
4. Macau's identity is not a fixed fusion, but a route-based experience.

## 结尾句

The value of our fieldwork was that movement across spaces revealed multiple versions of Macau.

## Q&A 快速备答

**Why no interviews?**  
We focused on observable public spaces. This is a limitation, but it also makes our method clear and honest.

**Why not Chinese-Portuguese fusion?**  
Fusion is too general. Our route shows several different spatial scripts.

**How did you choose images/videos?**  
We selected evidence that supports claims: signs, routes, buildings, crowds, commodities, and interaction.

**What is the biggest limitation?**  
The route is limited and mostly reflects a student visitor perspective. Future research could add interviews and residential areas.
