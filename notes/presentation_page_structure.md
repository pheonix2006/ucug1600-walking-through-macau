# Presentation Page Structure

> 目标：PPT 不像旅游相册，而是一条有证据、有节奏、有结论的 fieldwork story。

## 总体原则

- 15 页左右。
- 每页一个 claim，一个主视觉证据。
- 不平均展示所有地点；素材服务论点。
- 多使用路线切换、动态视频和空间对比。
- Q&A 准备方法、局限、素材选择和理论解释，不放无关问题。

## Slide 1. Title

**Title:** Curating Macau  
**Subtitle:** How One Route Stages Intercultural Identity  
**Visual:** 开场 montage：口岸、街道、大三巴、威尼斯人、NBA 计分屏。

**Claim:** Macau is not one fixed cultural image; it changes as we move.

## Slide 2. Route

**Visual:** 路线图/节点图。  
**Claim:** Our route moved through four versions of Macau.

节点：Border → Heritage → Street → Spectacle.

## Slide 3. Method

**Visual:** 四个方法标签 + 小图。  
**Claim:** We studied observable public space, not private opinions.

方法：

- Direct observation
- Photo and video documentation
- Walking route analysis
- Sensory observation
- No formal interviews

## Slide 4. Research Question

**Claim:** The question is not “what culture is Macau,” but how spaces stage Macau.

英文问题：

How do Macau's urban spaces stage different intercultural identities through routes, architecture, signs, sound, crowds, commodities, and interactive experiences?

## Slide 5. Framework

**Visual:** 四象限/路线框架。

| Space | Macau staged as |
|---|---|
| Threshold | Regulated entry |
| Heritage | Religious memory and history |
| Street | Everyday tourism commerce |
| Spectacle | Global consumer experience |

## Slide 6. Threshold Macau

**Visual:** 04 口岸大厅 + 05 排队区。  
**Claim:** Before seeing landmarks, we first experienced Macau as a controlled entry.

讲：导向、栏杆、排队、接驳车。

## Slide 7. Mobility

**Visual:** 07 车窗 + 33 公交 + 69 返程车厢。  
**Claim:** Movement was not empty time; it was part of the fieldwork.

讲：路线让空间差异变得可见。

## Slide 8. Heritage I

**Visual:** 25 大三巴 + 24 广场。  
**Claim:** Heritage is arranged for viewing, photographing, and remembering.

讲：牌坊不是单独出现，而在广场、台阶和人流中被观看。

## Slide 9. Heritage II

**Visual:** 27 墓室 + 30 大炮台石门 + 59 观景。  
**Claim:** The heritage route moves from facade to religious memory to city view.

讲：从外立面、室内展陈、坡道到观景平台。

## Slide 10. Street Macau

**Visual:** 22 手信餐饮街 + 23 街道尽头大三巴。  
**Claim:** Streets translate heritage into everyday tourism commerce.

讲：招牌、人流、小吃、商品、框景。

## Slide 11. Sensory Layer

**Visual:** 四个声音/节奏片段。  
**Claim:** Sound and pace change with each space.

例子：

- 口岸：排队、广播、脚步
- 街道：叫卖、人流、店铺声
- 教堂/墓室：更安静、更慢
- NBA 店：计分屏、投篮、游戏反馈

## Slide 12. Spectacle I

**Visual:** 44 威尼斯人穹顶 + 45 导视牌 + 61/62 仿天空/运河。  
**Claim:** The Venetian designs a walkable European imagination inside Macau.

讲：不是历史留下来的欧洲，而是设计出来的沉浸式消费空间。

## Slide 13. Spectacle II

**Visual:** 40 NBA 商品 + 68 投篮计分屏。  
**Claim:** Global culture becomes merchandise, game rules, and body movement.

讲：从球衣/收藏品到计分、倒计时和投篮动作。

## Slide 14. Synthesis

**Visual:** 四列表格。

| Space | Evidence | Mechanism | Macau staged as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Threshold | 04/05/06 | Queue, signs, shuttle | Controlled entry |
| Heritage | 25/27/30/59 | Facade, crypt, path, view | Historical memory |
| Street | 17/22/23 | Signs, food, crowd | Tourism commerce |
| Spectacle | 44/45/40/68 | Design, wayfinding, game | Global experience |

**Claim:** Macau's identity changes with the spatial script.

## Slide 15. Conclusion / Q&A

**Claim:** Macau's intercultural identity is not a fixed fusion, but a route-based experience.

结尾句：

The value of our fieldwork was that movement across spaces revealed multiple versions of Macau.

## Q&A 备答方向

1. **Why did you not use interviews?**  
   因为本项目聚焦公共空间中可观察的符号、动线和场景。无访谈是局限，但方法上诚实。

2. **Why not simply call Macau “Chinese-Portuguese fusion”?**  
   因为素材显示不同空间呈现不同澳门：口岸、遗产、街道和路氹奇观并不是同一种融合。

3. **How did you choose materials?**  
   选择能支撑论点的空间证据，优先建筑、标识、人流、商品和互动设施。

4. **What is the biggest limitation?**  
   路线有限，且主要是学生/游客视角；未来可加入居民访谈和更多社区路线。
